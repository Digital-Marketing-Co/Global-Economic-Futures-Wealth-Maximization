# Project Charter

## Purpose

Create one unified, publication-grade economic encyclopedia and interactive web platform for 249 statistical economies. The work must explain economic history, present conditions, sector structure, flows, risks, policy institutions, leading signals, and transparent future scenarios.

## Audience

Executives, investors, policymakers, economists, researchers, educators, and internationally oriented businesses requiring comparable country intelligence with direct provenance.

## Core coverage for every economy

1. Historical development and structural turning points
2. Geography, population, labor, human capital, institutions, and political economy
3. National accounts, productivity, income, wealth, inequality, and living standards
4. Inflation, wages, employment, exchange rates, interest rates, yield curves, credit, and financial conditions
5. Fiscal revenue, spending, balance, debt, assets, contingent liabilities, and sovereign risk
6. Monetary base and money aggregates, banking, payments, liquidity, reserves, and central-bank frameworks
7. Balance of payments, international investment position, capital flows, remittances, aid, and external vulnerability
8. Goods and services trade by partner, product, value chain, concentration, complexity, and terms of trade
9. Agriculture, extraction, manufacturing, construction, utilities, logistics, commerce, finance, technology, public services, and informal activity
10. Energy, resources, food, water, infrastructure, climate exposure, security, and geopolitical dependencies
11. Business formation, regulation, taxation, competition, innovation, digitalization, investment conditions, and market opportunities
12. Baseline, upside, downside, and stress scenarios with confidence ranges and explicit assumptions

## Publication architecture

- One global introduction of fewer than 20 pages
- One uniquely introduced and consistently structured dossier per economy
- One data appendix and provenance ledger
- One interactive country route per economy
- Comparative dashboards, maps, charts, and downloadable tables

## Visual direction

The global system uses deep navy, electric blue, cyan, restrained gold, and scholarly typography. Each economy inherits accessible accents from its flag. Flags may animate gently, while parallax and device-orientation effects remain optional, permission-gated, reduced-motion aware, and decorative rather than data-bearing.

## Boundary of certainty

No database contains every requested indicator for every economy and year. Completeness therefore means that every field in the declared ontology is attempted, sourced, and assigned a status: observed, estimated by the publisher, derived, scenario, unavailable, not applicable, or definitionally incomparable.
